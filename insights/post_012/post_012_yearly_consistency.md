# Quant Insight #012 — 132 Configs Tested. Only 1 Was Profitable Every Single Year.

**Chart:** `post_012_yearly_consistency.png`  
**Research base:** 132 parameter configurations · 6yr yearly screening  
**Data:** 6yr Binance USDT-M · 60 symbols · 2020-2025 · zero-losing-year criterion

---

## Key Finding

132 configurations screened with three filters: PF > 1.0, trade count > 1,000, and no losing years across all 6.

| Filter | Passed |
|--------|--------|
| PF > 1.0 + N > 1,000 | 68 / 132 |
| + Zero losing years | **1 / 132** |

The single surviving configuration:

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Worst year | 2023: **+50.1%** |
| Best year | 2020: **+517.9%** |
| Max intra-year DD | < 32.4% (every year) |
| 6yr Calmar | **5.8** |

---

## Why 2022 and 2023 Are the Key Filters

**2022**: Luna + FTX — sharp drawdowns testing risk controls  
**2023**: Low-momentum environment — edge had to survive without bull market tailwind

Configurations that relied on 2021 trending conditions typically failed 2023. The yearly constraint eliminates strategies that look good because they happened to catch one exceptional year.

---

## Methodology Note

Ranking by full-period return alone ignores how concentrated those returns were.  
A 6yr Calmar of 5.8 with zero losing years is structurally different from 5.8 Calmar that required one exceptional year to hold up.

**Caveat:** 6 years includes the most exceptional bull market in crypto history (2020–2021). Zero-losing-year performance over this window doesn't guarantee consistency in future regimes.
